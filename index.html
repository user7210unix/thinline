    syntax

    One rule per line, regular expressions in /slashes/flags, or plain text for a case-insensitive match. Lines starting with # are ignored. Options go after the pattern, separated by ; or spaces.

    • boards:a,jp limits to boards, exclude:vg removes boards, sfw and nsfw work too
    • op:only or op:no matches threads or replies only
    • file:only or file:no matches posts with or without a file
    • type:subject,comment picks fields, default subject,name,filename,comment. Join with + to test fields together
    • highlight, highlight:class, top:yes, notify, stub:yes, stub:no
    • Fields: postID, name, uniqueID, tripcode, capcode, pass, email, subject, comment, flag, filename, dimensions, filesize, MD5
